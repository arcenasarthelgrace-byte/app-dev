# My Favorite Series: Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir

## Overview
* **Title:** Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir
* **Genre:** Superhero / Action / Fantasy / Romance
* **Setting:** Paris, France

## Why I Like It
> "Best storytelling about two superheroes!"

This series features an engaging mix of superhero action, magical artifacts (Miraculouses), and dramatic secret-identity dynamics between Marinette and Adrien. The transformation sequences, creative villain designs, and ongoing mystery surrounding Hawk Moth keep every episode exciting.

### Main Characters & Kwamis
1. **Marinette Dupain-Cheng / Ladybug** (Kwami: *Tikki*)
2. **Adrien Agreste / Cat Noir** (Kwami: *Plagg*)
